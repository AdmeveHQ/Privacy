Admeve HQ — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

1. What this application is

Admeve HQ is a private, personal-use application. It is not distributed to the public and has exactly one user: the owner of the Google account it is authorized against (hermes.admeve@gmail.com). There is no sign-up, no public download, and no third-party users.

2. Who operates it

Operated by Fernando Figueredo ("the owner") on a privately controlled server. Contact: hermes.admeve@gmail.com

3. What Google data it accesses

Only after the owner grants OAuth consent, and only for the account hermes.admeve@gmail.com:

  • Google Drive — files and folders in that account, and items explicitly shared with it.
  • Google Docs — create documents, read documents, append text.
  • Google Sheets — create spreadsheets, read values, update cell ranges.
  • Google Calendar — read and manage events on that account's calendars.

It does not access Gmail, Contacts, or any other Google service. It cannot reach the owner's separate personal Google account unless the owner explicitly shares specific files or calendars with the automation account.

4. Purpose

To allow an AI executive assistant to read, organize, draft and update the owner's own documents, spreadsheets and calendar on his behalf, under his direction and his explicit approval.

5. Third parties and data sharing

No Google user data is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Third parties and data sharing

No Google user data is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Document content the assistant is asked to work on may be processed by third-party large-language-model API providers (OpenRouter and the underlying model vendors) solely to produce the requested output. Infrastructure providers used to host the assistant process data in the same limited way. Google user data is not used to train models by the owner, and no Google user data is used for advertising or profiling.

6. Retention

OAuth access and refresh tokens are retained until revoked. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends all API access; local credentials are then deleted. Working copies of documents are kept only as long as needed to complete the task at hand.

7. Security

The Google account password is never stored, requested, or transmitted. Access uses Google OAuth 2.0 with limited scopes. Tokens are held on a privately controlled server with file-level permissions restricted to the service account (Unix mode 600). The Google account is protected with two-factor authentication.

8. Children

This application is not directed to children and has no end users other than the account owner.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time; the effective date above will be revised accordingly.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or data handling: hermes.admeve@gmail.com
